# 📥 How to Get an Android APK for AINA Converter

Your AINA Converter is live at: `https://aina-converter-app.onrender.com`

Follow these steps to get a real `.apk` file you can install:

---

## 🪜 STEP 1 — Open PWABuilder

Open this URL in Chrome on your computer (or phone):
**https://www.pwabuilder.com**

---

## 🪜 STEP 2 — Enter your AINA URL

1. You'll see a big input box: **"Enter your PWA URL"**
2. Paste: `https://aina-converter-app.onrender.com`
3. Click **"Start"** (or press Enter)

---

## 🪜 STEP 3 — Wait for the analysis

- PWABuilder will check your site
- You should see a green checkmark for: PWA ready ✓
- If you see red ✗, the deploy didn't complete yet — wait 1-2 min and retry

---

## 🪜 STEP 4 — Click "Package for stores"

- You'll see a section: **"Package for stores"**
- Click on **"Android"** (the green Android robot icon)

---

## 🪜 STEP 5 — Configure (or just use defaults)

You'll see options like:
- Package ID: `com.aina.converter` (default is fine)
- App version: `1.0.0`
- Min Android version: `5.0` (default)

**Just click "Generate"** at the bottom

---

## 🪜 STEP 6 — Download

PWABuilder generates two files:
- 📦 **`.apk`** (Android Package) — for direct install
- 📦 **`.aab`** (Android App Bundle) — for Play Store

**Download the .apk** (the one you can install directly)

---

## 🪜 STEP 7 — Install on your phone

You have two options:

### Option A: Direct install (easiest)
1. Email the `.apk` to yourself
2. Open the email on your phone
3. Tap the `.apk` file
4. Android will ask: "Install from unknown source?"
5. Tap **"Settings"** → enable **"Allow from this source"**
6. Tap **"Install"**
7. **Done!** AINA icon appears in your app drawer

### Option B: Transfer via USB
1. Connect phone to computer
2. Copy `.apk` to phone's Downloads folder
3. Open file manager on phone
4. Tap the `.apk` file
5. Tap **"Install"**

---

## 🪜 STEP 8 — Use the app

- Open your app drawer (swipe up from home screen)
- Tap **"AINA Converter"**
- The app opens full-screen, like a real app
- No browser bar, no "Add to Home Screen" needed!

---

## 🎉 Done!

You now have a **real Android app** installed on your phone. Works exactly like any app from the Play Store.

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"App not installed" error:**
- Uninstall any old version first
- Make sure you allowed "Install from unknown sources"

**"Parse error - there was a problem parsing the package":**
- The APK is corrupted, re-download
- Your Android version is too old (need 5.0+)

**App crashes on launch:**
- Make sure the URL in the APK matches your live URL
- Check your Render static site is still Live
